## Smash the skeletons. Collect the treats. Don't smash the cat!

A fast, colourful **3D Halloween tap game** that runs in any browser — phones, tablets and desktops.
Re-skin it for any brand in minutes, run a leaderboard campaign, or publish it on YouTube Playables
and ad-supported game portals.

**▶ Play the demo:** <your demo link>

### Why it's useful
- **Brand campaigns:** change the name, logo, colours and the five collectible products (with real product
  photos) in one JSON file. Daily/weekly leaderboards and a one-click winners CSV make prize campaigns easy.
- **Publish & earn:** ready-made builds for **YouTube Playables**, **CrazyGames** and **GameDistribution**
  (ads between rounds) — one command each.
- **Your website:** works as plain static files, or with the included zero-dependency Node.js leaderboard server.

### Features
- Real-time 3D (three.js), portrait & landscape, automatic low-graphics mode
- Tap controls with forgiving hit areas; keyboard 1–9 on desktop
- Tutorial, countdown, pause, sound, vibration, fullscreen
- Skill-based scoring: combos, reaction speed, full sets, survival & lives bonus
- 3 skins included (treat bags, drinks cans, reward coins); 5 collectible shapes (bag, can, box, coin, gift)
- Optional mobile-number entry for prize campaigns
- Fully offline: fonts, models, effects and synthesized sound included
- HTML documentation
